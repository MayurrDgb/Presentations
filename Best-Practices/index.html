RATIONALE
The Why
A real customer need + a fragmented, slow local response = an offer that under-performs its potential. We turned it into one packaged go-to-market.
The market need
Logistics is a growing pain for merchants — and a high-value, recurring MBE offer (MBE Fulfillment).
72% of merchants spend 15h+ / week on order prep & shipping.
Peak-season temp staff costs +30–50%.
Source: e-commerce sector studies
The problem we hit
Each centre marketed the offer alone — inconsistent messages, slow production, no shared toolkit.
Source material on the offer was scattered, and there was no clear playbook to sell a complex, modular service.
Our answer
One packaged go-to-market: a cloud hub, a full ready-to-use asset pack, and personalised commercial materials.
Built once · delivered to all →
The best practice —
We shouldn't expect BSCs (Business Solution Centers) to know how to market the logistics offer. Package it centrally, keep it brand-safe, and deliver ready-to-use assets to every centre.
